History of Digital Shield

Digital Shield was started in 2025 as an educational cyber-awareness initiative after observing a rapid increase in phishing links, fake prize messages, and social media account hijackings among students and new internet users. Our mission is to explain digital safety in clear, easy-to-follow steps.

Key Milestones

  1. Phase I (2025): Collected real-world examples of fake links and online scams targeting everyday users.
  2. Phase II (Early 2026): Prepared awareness posters on Password Safety and Two-Factor Authentication.
  3. Phase III (Late 2026): Launched the complete Digital Shield educational portal.
