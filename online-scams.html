Online Scams & Fake Links

Online scams occur when fraudsters send fake website links, urgent messages, or deceptive phone calls to steal money or account credentials. Learning to recognize these warning signs helps users avoid financial and data loss.

Common Scams & Prevention Guide

Compare common online scams, how attackers trick users, and the right action to take:

Common Online Scams & How to Avoid Them
Scam Type How It Tricks Users What You Should Do
Fake Login Links (Phishing) A link mimics an official bank or student portal with misspelled domain names (e.g., portal-login-verify.com). Check spelling carefully and look for HTTPS.
Fake Verification Calls Caller claims your account will be blocked unless you share your 6-digit OTP code. Hang up immediately; legitimate organizations never ask for OTP.
Fake Scholarship / Job SMS Message asks for an advance registration fee to receive a free laptop or scholarship grant. Verify through official noticeboards and trusted portals only.
Lottery & Prize Messages Claims you won cash prizes in a lucky draw you never entered. Ignore, delete, and block the sender.

Red Flags Checklist

  • Check the Link Address First
    1. Avoid links starting with http:// when entering sensitive details; prefer https://.
    2. Look closely for extra letters or numbers in brand names.
    3. Do not click unknown shortened links sent in group chats.
  • Watch Out for Urgent Threat Messages
    • Messages claiming Your account will be suspended in 1 hour!
    • Requests asking for immediate money transfers to unknown accounts.
  • Never download unexpected .exe or .apk files from unverified websites.
