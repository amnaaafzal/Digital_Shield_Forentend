Password Safety & Two-Factor Authentication

Using simple passwords like 123456 or common words allows attackers to guess your password in seconds. A strong, unique password combined with Two-Factor Authentication keeps your email, banking, and personal accounts secure.

Password Strength Scale

See how different password patterns rank from dangerous to unbreakable:

10.0 — UNBREAKABLE: 16+ chars with passphrase, symbols & 2FA (e.g., S@fe2026#Shield!88)

9.0 — VERY STRONG: 14 chars with uppercase, lowercase, numbers & symbols

8.0 — STRONG: 12 mixed characters

7.0 — FAIR: 10 letters and numbers only

5.0 — WEAK: Dictionary word + birth year (e.g., student2005)

3.0 — VERY WEAK: Phone number or city name

0.0 — DANGEROUS: 12345678 or password (Guessed instantly!)

4 Practical Password Rules

  1. Make every password at least 12 characters long using A-Z, a-z, 0-9, and symbols (@ # $ % !).
  2. Do not use one password for every website; keep separate passwords for important accounts.
  3. Turn on Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on email and social media accounts.
  4. Always log out after using shared computers in labs or libraries.
Quick Example:
Weak Password   : user12345         (Easy to guess)
Strong Password : UsR@2026#Shd!91   (Hard to crack)
